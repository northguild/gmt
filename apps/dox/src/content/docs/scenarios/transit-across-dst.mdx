<Scenario
  title="A 26-Hour Rail Leg Across Spring-Forward"
  description="A train departs London at 20:00 on 30 March 2024 and its scheduled running time is 26 hours. Clocks spring forward at 01:00 that night. When does it arrive?"
  naiveCode={`// The naive approach: add a day and two hours to the wall clock
const departure = "2024-03-30T20:00:00"; // local wall time
// "one day later" is 2024-03-31T20:00:00, plus two hours is 22:00
const arrival = "2024-03-31T22:00:00";
// The train has run 25 elapsed hours at 22:00 on the 31st, not 26.
// The published arrival is an hour early, and every connection off it is wrong.`}
  explanation={`The night of 30 March 2024 is 23 hours long in London. "A day later" on the wall clock is only 23 elapsed hours, so a wall-clock addition of one day and two hours puts the train in the station after 25 hours of running, an hour before it is really there. A running time is *elapsed* time: the train does not go faster because the clocks changed. \`transitTime\` adds the duration as exact time in the departure's own zone, so a \`D\` component is 24 hours exactly and the arrival's wall clock shows whatever offset is in force when it gets there.`}
  gmtCode={`import { transitTime } from "@northguild/gmt/transport";

transitTime("2024-03-30T20:00:00+00:00[Europe/London]", "PT26H");
// "2024-03-31T23:00:00+01:00[Europe/London]" — 26 elapsed hours, BST on arrival

// P1DT2H is the same 26 hours: a day is 24 exact hours here, not "tomorrow"
transitTime("2024-03-30T20:00:00+00:00[Europe/London]", "P1DT2H");
// "2024-03-31T23:00:00+01:00[Europe/London]"`}
  fixedSpecId="transitTime"
/>

The wall-clock addition is what [`addZoned`](/reference/zoned/calculate/addZoned) does on
purpose: its date part moves the wall clock, which is right for "the same meeting next week"
and wrong for a running time. Both are correct; they answer different questions. See
[Recurring Meeting Across DST](/scenarios/recurring-meeting-dst/) for the case where the
wall clock is what should hold, and [Transport: Legs and
Dwell](/guides/industries/transport-legs-and-dwell/) for the rest of the leg rules.
