The widget above starts from the legs with no scheduled sailing: 400 hours moving and 84 at
handoffs. Add `departure: "2024-06-13T10:00:00+08:00[Asia/Shanghai]"` to the ship leg and
`totalDwell` grows to `PT104H` while `totalTransit` stays `PT400H`. Make it
`"2024-06-12T13:00:00+08:00[Asia/Shanghai]"`, inside the two-day dwell, and the result is `null`:
a missed connection. The full rules are in [Intermodal: B/L Dates and Multimodal
ETA](/guides/industries/intermodal-bill-of-lading-and-eta/).

To see where the time went, [open these legs in the Journey Split](/tools/journey-split/?w=journeysplit&wa=%7B%22legCount%22%3A%223%22%2C%22departure1%22%3A%222024-06-10T08%3A00%3A00%2B08%3A00%5BAsia%2FShanghai%5D%22%2C%22duration1%22%3A%22PT6H%22%2C%22timeZone1%22%3A%22Asia%2FShanghai%22%2C%22dwellAfter1%22%3A%22P2D%22%2C%22mode1%22%3A%22truck%22%2C%22departure2%22%3A%222024-06-13T10%3A00%3A00%2B08%3A00%5BAsia%2FShanghai%5D%22%2C%22duration2%22%3A%22P14DT6H%22%2C%22timeZone2%22%3A%22America%2FLos_Angeles%22%2C%22dwellAfter2%22%3A%22PT36H%22%2C%22mode2%22%3A%22ship%22%2C%22duration3%22%3A%22PT52H%22%2C%22timeZone3%22%3A%22America%2FChicago%22%2C%22mode3%22%3A%22rail%22%7D).
