---
title: "Two Days Before Is Not 48 Hours"
description: A documentation cut-off is 17:00 local two days before the vessel sails. Subtracting 48 hours from the departure puts it an hour late, and a DST weekend moves it again.
---

import Scenario from "~/components/Scenario.astro";

<Scenario
  title="Two Days Before Is Not 48 Hours"
  description="A vessel sails at 18:00 on Friday 14 June 2024 from Amsterdam. The tariff closes documentation at 17:00 local, two days before sailing. When is the cut-off?"
  naiveCode={`// The naive approach: two days is 48 hours
const sails = Date.parse("2024-06-14T16:00:00Z"); // Friday 18:00 in Amsterdam
const cutoff = new Date(sails - 48 * 36e5);
cutoff.toISOString(); // "2024-06-12T16:00:00.000Z" — Wednesday 18:00 in Amsterdam
// The terminal closed documentation at 17:00. Every filing made in that hour is late.`}
  explanation={`"Two days before, 17:00" names a day and a time of day on the terminal's clock, not a length of time. Subtracting 48 hours keeps the departure's own time of day, 18:00, so the cut-off is an hour late whenever the vessel sails at any time other than 17:00. When a DST transition falls between the two dates, 48 hours also lands on a different wall-clock hour. The pinned cut-off keeps its 17:00 and lets the elapsed time move instead: before a Monday 18:00 departure in New York, across the 10 March 2024 spring-forward, it is 48 elapsed hours earlier, not 49. \`cutoffAt\` with \`atLocalTime\` takes the two days off the local date and pins the time of day, so the cut-off is 17:00 on the terminal's clock whatever the departure time or the DST calendar.`}
  gmtCode={`import { cutoffAt } from "@northguild/gmt/transport";

cutoffAt("2024-06-14T16:00:00Z", "P2D", { timeZone: "Europe/Amsterdam", atLocalTime: "17:00" });
// "2024-06-12T17:00:00+02:00[Europe/Amsterdam]" — Wednesday 17:00, 49 hours before sailing

cutoffAt("2024-06-14T16:00:00Z", "PT48H", { timeZone: "Europe/Amsterdam" });
// "2024-06-12T18:00:00+02:00[Europe/Amsterdam]" — what subtracting 48 hours gives

// Across the spring-forward the local time holds and the elapsed time moves.
cutoffAt("2024-03-11T22:00:00Z", "P2D", { timeZone: "America/New_York", atLocalTime: "17:00" });
// "2024-03-09T17:00:00-05:00[America/New_York]" — 48 elapsed hours before Monday 18:00 EDT`}
  fixedSpecId="cutoffAt"
/>

In the playground, change `atLocalTime` to `"10:00"` for a VGM cut-off, or remove it to see the
cut-off keep the departure's 18:00. Add
`calendar: { weekend: [6, 7], holidays: ["2024-06-12"], timeZone: "Europe/Amsterdam" }, roll: "preceding"`
to make Wednesday a holiday: the cut-off rolls back to Tuesday. Change `roll` to `"following"`
and it moves to Thursday. Leave `roll` out and the result is `""`, because there is no default. The rules are
in [Transport: Legs and
Dwell](/guides/industries/transport-legs-and-dwell/#two-days-before-is-not-48-hours-cutoffat).
