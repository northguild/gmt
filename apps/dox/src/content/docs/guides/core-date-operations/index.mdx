Everything you do with plain dates and datetimes in GMT.

- [Durations](./durations/) — parse, validate, combine, rebalance, and format ISO 8601 duration strings.
- [Comparisons](./comparisons/) — order, equality, weekends, business days, and now-relative predicates.
- [Formatting](./formatting/) — locale-aware display, relative labels, calendar-day labels, and fixed machine formats.
- [Parsing](./parsing/) — extract components from ISO strings and decode interchange grammars.
- [Validation](./validation/) — validate date/time strings, timezone identifiers, intervals, and ranges.
- [Calendar Systems](./calendar-systems/) — express dates in non-Gregorian calendars with native arithmetic.
