Proposals under this name have been put forward in several sessions; this page describes what they would change, not any one text. As of 13 November 2026, most of the United States changes its clocks twice a year, Arizona (apart from the Navajo Nation), Hawaii and the territories keep one time all year, and many states have adopted a position on what they would do if federal law allowed a change.
