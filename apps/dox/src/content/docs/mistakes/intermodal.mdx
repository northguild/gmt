---
title: Intermodal
description: Common mistakes with free time, demurrage and detention, billing deadlines, bill of lading dates, multimodal ETAs and EDI timestamps in GMT's intermodal layer.
industries: [intermodal]
---

The intermodal layer's functions fall into four groups. Free time and demurrage:
`freeTimeExpiry`, `chargeableDays` and `demurrageClock`. Billing deadlines: `billingTimeline`.
B/L dates and multimodal ETA: `bolTimestamp` and `multimodalETA`. EDI timestamps:
`parseEdifactDtm`, `formatEdifactDtm`, `isValidEdifactDtm`, `isValidEdifactDtmFormat`,
`parseX12DateTime`, `isValidX12DateTime`, `parseX12DateTimePeriod`, `formatX12DateTimePeriod`,
`isValidX12DateTimePeriod`, `isValidX12DateTimePeriodFormat`, `x12TimeCode`,
`isValidX12TimeCode`, `parseEpcisEvent`, `formatEpcisEvent` and `isValidEpcisEvent`.

The mistakes around them are the ones that turn into disputed invoices and documents: a defaulted
start day, a free-time table keyed by port, a clock counted from the wrong event, a day count
taken from elapsed hours, a deadline window hard-coded or counted from the wrong date, a B/L
dated in UTC, an ETA that forgets the schedule, an offsetless code read as UTC, a named time code
treated as an offset, an X12 date and time joined by hand, and a two-digit year given a century by
the library. Every output shown
here is what the function returns.

<Mistake

## EDI timestamps
