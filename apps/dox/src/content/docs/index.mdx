<div class="gmt-home-lead" data-page="About gmt">

Current release: **v{gmtVersion}**

`@northguild/gmt` is built on the published standards for dates and times, not on a
library's own idea of them: [TC39 Temporal](https://tc39.es/proposal-temporal/) for the
types and arithmetic, [ISO 8601](https://www.iso.org/iso-8601-date-and-time-format.html)
and [RFC 9557](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9557) for every string in and out, and
the IANA Time Zone Database for zones. On top of that sits a smaller, opinionated API for
what application code actually needs — arithmetic, comparison, parsing, formatting, unix
conversions, timezone conversion and validation — tested against those standards and exact
to the nanosecond at the edges of Temporal's range. No hardcoded offset tables, no `Date` object: every zone on
the globe is computed live in your browser and recomputed every second.
