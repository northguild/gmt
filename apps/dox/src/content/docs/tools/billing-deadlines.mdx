Each deadline below is a date, counted in calendar days from day zero, with
the deadline day itself inside the window. There is no clock time to it and
no instant to compare against — only whole dates, on the ISO calendar.

The three windows — issue, dispute, resolve — are yours, and none is
defaulted: a blank window is left out of the call rather than filled in, and
the result names which one is missing and says why it is `null`. Lane 1 is the
issue window, lane 2 the dispute window and lane 3 the resolution window, each
drawn with its own pattern and number so the strip reads without relying on
colour alone; the anchor, the invoice date and the request date are each
lettered.

The strip and the verdicts below are the real
[`billingTimeline`](/reference/intermodal/calculate/billingTimeline/) call for
what is on screen. Nothing here computes a deadline — it only draws what the
function returned.

one day past the 14-day dispute deadline, and the request verdict turns to
after the deadline while the resolution deadline stays `null`.

Reference: [`billingTimeline`](/reference/intermodal/calculate/billingTimeline/)
lays out the chain; [`convertUtcToPlainDate`](/reference/utc/convert/convertUtcToPlainDate/)
reduces an instant to the billing party's local date before it is passed in.
The rules for reading the strip are in
[Intermodal: Billing Deadlines](/guides/industries/intermodal-billing-deadlines/).
