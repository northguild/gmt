<Fragment slot="keypoint">

A billing deadline is a date, not an instant. Each one is counted in calendar days from day zero, and the deadline day itself is inside the window.

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="intro">

The three windows — issue, dispute, resolve — are yours, and none is
defaulted. A blank window is left out of the call, and the result names which
one is missing and says why it is `null`.

Lane 1 is the issue window, lane 2 the dispute window and lane 3 the resolution
window. Each has its own pattern and number, and the anchor, the invoice date
and the request date are each lettered, so the strip reads without relying on
colour. The strip draws what [`billingTimeline`](/reference/intermodal/calculate/billingTimeline/) returned; nothing here
computes a deadline.

</Fragment>

the invoice deadline does not move, but the verdict turns to after the
deadline. Then [a 14/14/45 contract with the request moved to 20
March](/tools/billing-deadlines/?w=billing&wa=%7B%22anchorOn%22%3A%222026-03-01%22%2C%22invoiceIssuedOn%22%3A%222026-03-05%22%2C%22requestReceivedOn%22%3A%222026-03-20%22%2C%22issueDays%22%3A%2214%22%2C%22disputeDays%22%3A%2214%22%2C%22resolutionDays%22%3A%2245%22%7D) —
one day past the 14-day dispute deadline, and the request verdict turns to
after the deadline while the resolution deadline stays `null`.

Reference: [`billingTimeline`](/reference/intermodal/calculate/billingTimeline/)
lays out the chain; [`convertUtcToPlainDate`](/reference/utc/convert/convertUtcToPlainDate/)
reduces an instant to the billing party's local date before it is passed in.
The rules for reading the strip are in
[Intermodal: Billing Deadlines](/guides/industries/intermodal-billing-deadlines/).
