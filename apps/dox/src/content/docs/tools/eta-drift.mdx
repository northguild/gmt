One field often holds four kinds of time. DCSA's vocabulary names them: PLN
planned, EST estimated, REQ requested and ACT actual. `bestAvailable` picks
one and returns its class with it, so a column can say what it shows. The
newest record is not the best one.

Drift is the last estimate minus the first. It exceeds a tolerance only when
it is greater than it, in either direction. Drag the tolerance and watch
`exceedsTolerance` change.

<EtaDrift fullbleed />

**Worth trying:** [an estimate recorded after the
actual](/tools/eta-drift/?w=etadrift&wa=%7B%22preset%22%3A%22est-after-act%22%7D)
rings the actual as the best available and the late estimate as the naive pick.
Then [a tolerance of exactly 9
hours](/tools/eta-drift/?w=etadrift&wa=%7B%22preset%22%3A%22vessel-slide%22%2C%22tolerance%22%3A%22PT9H%22%7D):
the drift is 9 hours, equal does not exceed, and `exceedsTolerance` is false.
