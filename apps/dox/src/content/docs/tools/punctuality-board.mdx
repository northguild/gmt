The tolerance is yours. GMT holds no default, because "on time" means nothing
until someone states how late is too late. Both edges belong to the outside: an
arrival exactly on the late edge is late, and one exactly on the early edge is
early. Drag the band and watch the rows change their word.

A rate travels with its tolerance. The same six arrivals are 2 of 6 on time
under one tolerance and 4 of 6 under another. Turn on the second tolerance to
see both at once.
