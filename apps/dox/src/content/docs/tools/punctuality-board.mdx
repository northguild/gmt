<Fragment slot="keypoint">

On time means nothing until someone states how late is too late, so GMT holds no default tolerance. A rate travels with its tolerance: the same six arrivals are 2 of 6 on time under one and 4 of 6 under another.

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="intro">

Both edges belong to the outside: an arrival exactly on the late edge is
late, and one exactly on the early edge is early. Drag the band and watch the
rows change their word.

Turn on the second tolerance to see both rates at once.

</Fragment>
