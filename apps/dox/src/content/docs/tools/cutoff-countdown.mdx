<Fragment slot="keypoint">

The window to meet a cut-off is half-open: open right up to the cut-off instant, and closed from that instant on. There is no in-between state at the deadline.

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="intro">

`isPastCutoff` is `true` and `timeToCutoff` is `PT0S` at the cut-off itself,
not a moment before it. Drag "now" past the cut-off and watch on time turn to
late; drag it back and watch late turn to on time.

</Fragment>
