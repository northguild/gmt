<Fragment slot="keypoint">

A connection is made when the onward departure is at or after the arrival plus the handling time. Exactly on it is made, with zero slack. Before it is missed, and `scheduleDelivery` returns `null`.

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="intro">

One handoff: an inbound leg, a handling time at the port — the minimum
connect time — and a scheduled onward departure. Slide the handling time and
watch made turn to missed.

Beside the real verdict is the naive check of the times as printed: a
wall-clock addition with no zone and no DST. It agrees when nothing moves the
port's offset between the two ends, and disagrees when a DST transition or a
zone change does.

</Fragment>
