One handoff: an inbound leg, a handling time at the port — the minimum
connect time — and a scheduled onward departure. `scheduleDelivery` checks
the onward departure against the arrival plus the handling time; a departure
before that is a missed connection, and returns `null`. A departure exactly
on it is a made connection with zero slack: equal passes.

Slide the handling time and watch made turn to missed. Beside the real
verdict is the naive check of the times as printed — a wall-clock addition
with no zone and no DST — which agrees when nothing moves the port's offset
between the two ends, and disagrees when a DST transition or a zone change
does.
