title: Crossing Clock

---

A crossing — a canal transit, a strait passage, a border queue — is logged
as two instants and read on the clock of whoever administers it.
`crossingTime` gives the exact elapsed hours, with the entry and exit
rendered in that one zone. Subtracting the two ends' printed wall clocks
instead gives the wrong answer whenever a DST transition falls between
them.

Entry and exit each get their own analog face, reading the local time,
offset, zone and date `crossingTime` returned — never a second computation.
Between them, the true elapsed duration sits above the naive wall-clock
subtraction, flagged when the two disagree. A skipped or repeated hour is
drawn right on both faces, as a sector between the tick ring and the
centre — dashed for an hour that never showed, doubled for one the clock
ran through twice — and the same hour is marked on the strip below the
clocks, so the dial and the bar always agree. On the fall-back night, both
faces read identically and are marked repeated, so identical clocks don't
read as a bug.

**Worth trying:** the default preset already shows a skipped hour — New
York's clocks jump from 02:00 to 03:00, seven hours actually pass, and
subtracting the wall clocks says eight. Switch to [the added-hour
preset](/tools/crossing-clock/?w=crossing&wa=%7B%22entry%22%3A%222024-11-03T05%3A30%3A00Z%22%2C%22exit%22%3A%222024-11-03T06%3A30%3A00Z%22%2C%22targetZone%22%3A%22America%2FNew_York%22%7D)
to see the opposite: one hour actually passes, but both ends read 01:30, so
subtracting the wall clocks says zero. Then [the same spring-forward
night read on a fixed
−05:00](/tools/crossing-clock/?w=crossing&wa=%7B%22entry%22%3A%222024-03-10T05%3A00%3A00Z%22%2C%22exit%22%3A%222024-03-10T12%3A00%3A00Z%22%2C%22targetZone%22%3A%22-05%3A00%22%7D) —
a fixed offset observes no DST, so the wall-clock subtraction agrees and no
sector is drawn at all.

A day count for a stay is [`dwellTime`](/reference/transport/calculate/dwellTime/),
drawn in the [Dwell Ledger](/tools/dwell-ledger/).
