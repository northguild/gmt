<Fragment slot="keypoint">

The threshold is your arrival plus the minimum connection. A departure exactly at the threshold is made. One a minute earlier is gone, even though it is on the timetable.

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="intro">

Drag the arrival and watch the hatched connection bar push the answer to the
next departure.

A service every N minutes is three values: the gap between departures, a
start and an end. The end is never a departure. The window is
half-open, so a shuttle every 20 minutes from 06:00 to 09:00 leaves last at
08:40.

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="after">

**Worth trying:** [arriving exactly at the end of the
service window](/tools/departure-board/?w=departure&wa=%7B%22preset%22%3A%22arrival-at-to%22%7D)
leaves no departure, and that is a correct answer, not a failure. Then [the
ferry list with no connection
time](/tools/departure-board/?w=departure&wa=%7B%22preset%22%3A%22ferry-list%22%2C%22minimumConnection%22%3A%22none%22%7D):
the 10:30 is made, and the connection time changes nothing.

Reference: [`nextDeparture`](/reference/transport/calculate/nextDeparture/)
finds the departure and
[`scheduleDelivery`](/reference/transport/calculate/scheduleDelivery/) takes it
on. See the guide's
[next departure section](/guides/industries/transport-punctuality-and-timestamps/#the-next-departure-you-can-make-nextdeparture),
and the [Connection Checker](/tools/connection-checker/) for one handoff's
handling time.
