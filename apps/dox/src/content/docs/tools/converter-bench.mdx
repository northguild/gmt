Three benches over one instant: convert it between zones, format it in any locale
`@northguild/gmt` is tested against, and check which of the library's exported
regular expressions match a given string.

<ConverterBench />

**Worth trying:** change the locale to `ar-SA` or `ja-JP` and watch the formatted
parts change shape, not just language — the same instant, ordered the way that
locale orders dates.

Reference:
[`convertZonedToZoned`](/reference/zoned/convert/convertZonedToZoned/) does the
conversion; [`formatZonedToParts`](/reference/zoned/format/formatZonedToParts/)
and [`formatRelativeZoned`](/reference/zoned/format/formatRelativeZoned/) do the
formatting.
