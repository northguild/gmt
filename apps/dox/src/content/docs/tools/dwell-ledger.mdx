Terminals, yards, gates and berths charge dwell by the local calendar days
something was present, not by elapsed hours. A container that gates in at 23:00
and out at 01:00 has been there two hours and two days, and dividing hours by 24
can never see the midnight in between.

The track below is one zone's local days, each cell as wide as that day really
is: New York's 10 March 2024 had 23 hours, so its cell is narrower. The bar is
the elapsed time. Shaded cells are the dates the dwell touched, counted
half-open: an exit exactly at local midnight does not touch the new day. The
output is the real
[`dwellTime`](/reference/transport/calculate/dwellTime/) call for what is on
screen.

**Worth trying:** [the same two instants in London and
Amsterdam](/tools/dwell-ledger/?w=dwell&wa=%7B%22entry%22%3A%222024-06-15T22%3A30%3A00Z%22%2C%22exit%22%3A%222024-06-16T01%3A00%3A00Z%22%2C%22zone%22%3A%22Europe%2FLondon%22%2C%22compareZone%22%3A%22Europe%2FAmsterdam%22%7D).
The bar is identical on both rows, and the count is two days in London and one
in Amsterdam, because Amsterdam's midnight falls before the entry. Then [drop
the zone](/tools/dwell-ledger/?w=dwell&wa=%7B%22entry%22%3A%222024-06-15T22%3A30%3A00Z%22%2C%22exit%22%3A%222024-06-16T01%3A00%3A00Z%22%7D):
two bare instants name no place, an offset is not a zone, and `dwellTime`
returns `null` rather than guess.

Reference: [`dwellTime`](/reference/transport/calculate/dwellTime/) counts the
days across the zone's real transitions;
[`floorToZone`](/reference/calendar/calculate/floorToZone/) finds the start of
any local day. The rules are in [Transport: Legs and
Dwell](/guides/industries/transport-legs-and-dwell/).
