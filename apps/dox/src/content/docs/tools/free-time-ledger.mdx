Free time is counted in the terminal's local days, and none of its terms is a
fact about the port. Whether the day of discharge is free day one, whether
weekends count, and how many days are free are set by the tariff, and the two
start-day conventions alone differ by a full day of charges.

The track below is one zone's local days, each cell as wide as that day really
is. Free days are green, chargeable days amber, and on a working-day tariff the
closed days are hatched and not counted. The bar is the dwell, half-open at the
gate-out: leaving exactly at the expiry line charges nothing. The outputs are
the real [`freeTimeExpiry`](/reference/intermodal/calculate/freeTimeExpiry/) and
[`chargeableDays`](/reference/intermodal/calculate/chargeableDays/) calls for
what is on screen, including the charged dates.

**Worth trying:** [the same Friday discharge read as
`nextDay`](/tools/free-time-ledger/?w=freetime&wa=%7B%22clockStart%22%3A%222024-06-14T19%3A00%3A00Z%22%2C%22clockEnd%22%3A%222024-06-17T04%3A00%3A01Z%22%2C%22freeDays%22%3A%223%22%2C%22firstDay%22%3A%22nextDay%22%2C%22basis%22%3A%22calendar%22%2C%22zone%22%3A%22America%2FNew_York%22%7D).
The bar does not move, but the free window slides a day and the Monday gate-out
is no longer charged. Then [gate out exactly at the
expiry](/tools/free-time-ledger/?w=freetime&wa=%7B%22clockStart%22%3A%222024-06-14T19%3A00%3A00Z%22%2C%22clockEnd%22%3A%222024-06-17T04%3A00%3A00Z%22%2C%22freeDays%22%3A%223%22%2C%22firstDay%22%3A%22eventDay%22%2C%22basis%22%3A%22calendar%22%2C%22zone%22%3A%22America%2FNew_York%22%7D)
and step the end handle one notch right: the dwell is half-open, so that
instant is free and the next is not.

Reference: [`freeTimeExpiry`](/reference/intermodal/calculate/freeTimeExpiry/)
lays out the free window; [`chargeableDays`](/reference/intermodal/calculate/chargeableDays/)
counts and lists the charged days; both count from the same local-day boundary
as [`dwellTime`](/reference/transport/calculate/dwellTime/). The rules are in
[Intermodal: Free Time and Demurrage](/guides/industries/intermodal-free-time-and-demurrage/).
