# Domination specs

Implementation specs that sit beside the per-story files in [issues/index.md](../issues/index.md).
A spec exists only while its story is in progress. When the story is Done, the finalizer folds its
decisions into `issues/<ID>.md` under `## Decisions` and deletes the spec.
