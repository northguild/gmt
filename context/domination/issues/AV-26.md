# AV-26 — Aviation: Schedule seasons, SSIM dates and connections

**Scope:** IATA scheduling seasons, SSIM date, time-variation and day-of-operation encodings, and minimum connect time.

## Gap

Airline scheduling is organised around seasons, and GMT could not express one. Every schedule, slot allocation and timetable is filed against a season, and the season boundaries are deliberately aligned to European DST — so they move each year and cannot be hardcoded as dates. The coordinators' own guide states the rule: "The summer season runs from the last Sunday in March to the Saturday before the last Sunday in October", and "slot requests must be made using the UTC time".

Alongside that, SSIM carries its own encodings that no general date library parses: `DDMMMYY` dates, a seven-character days-of-operation string ("Monday is indicated by 1, Tuesday by 2, etc."; "The days on which there is no operation are represented by a 0 in the corresponding position"), a five-character UTC/local time variation on every leg, and a date-variation digit that says the arrival is on a different day from the departure.

([AECFA, *Quick Guide to Using the IATA SSIM format*, Chapter 6 slot messages](https://www.slotcoordination.es/sites/Satellite?blobcol=urldata&blobkey=id&blobtable=MungoBlobs&blobwhere=1576865330896&ssbinary=true); [IATA SSIM manual](https://www.iata.org/en/publications/manuals/standard-schedules-information/) — paid; the Chapter 7 record-type-3 column layout below is corroborated by two independent public parsers and is labelled secondary until checked against the manual)

## IATA scheduling seasons

- **Northern Summer (NS)** runs from the last Sunday in March to the Saturday before the last Sunday in October.
- **Northern Winter (NW)** covers the remainder of the year.
- Boundaries coincide with EU daylight saving transitions, which is why they are computed rather than tabulated.
- Written with the trailing two year digits, e.g. `NS26`; a winter season spanning a year boundary is often written `NW25/26`.

## Design notes

- **Season boundaries are computed from the rule, not stored.** The last Sunday in March and October come from CORE-54's `nthWeekdayOfMonth`; hardcoding dates guarantees the library is wrong every year.
- **`DDMMMYY` month abbreviations are English and locale-independent.** They must not go through `Intl`, or a non-English default locale silently breaks parsing. This is an explicit exception to the library's locale-aware formatting rules and belongs in the JSDoc.
- **The two-digit year needs a pivot, and it is the caller's.** SSIM files carry schedules years ahead and archives decades back; no pivot is safe for both, so `centuryPivot` is required, as it is for NMEA (MAR-59) and SWIFT (FIN-73).
- **Time variation and date variation are how SSIM says what `flightLeg` computes.** A leg is published as local times with a UTC variation and a day digit; `ssimLegTimes` turns that into the two instants AV-25 then compares, and a round-trip through `flightLeg` must reproduce the digit. That test is what catches a sign error in the variation.
- **`minimumConnect` is a parameter.** MCT varies by airport, terminal pair, domestic/international status and carrier, and no bundled table would be correct.
- The record-type-3 column positions (period of operation 15–28, days of operation 29–35, aircraft STD 44–47, departure variation 48–52, arrival variation 66–70, date variation 193–194) are documented in the JSDoc as secondary until the manual is checked; the parsers here take fields, not fixed-width records, so a column correction changes no signature.

## What gmt provides (do not re-implement)

- `getIsoWeekDate` / `floorToZone` from CORE-5 — weekday and boundary arithmetic
- `nthWeekdayOfMonth` from CORE-54 — the last Sunday in March and October
- `resolveLocal` from CORE-4 — season boundary instants
- `toOffsetInstant` from CORE-4 — leg times with their variation
- `spanMs` from CORE-2 — connection time
- `flightLeg` from AV-25 — the round-trip check on date variation

## Verification

- `getScheduleSeason` returns `NS` for a date in April and `NW` for a date in November
- A date on the last Sunday in March falls in `NS`; the preceding Saturday falls in `NW`; the Saturday before the last Sunday in October is the last day of `NS`
- Season boundaries coincide with the EU DST transition for the year under test, asserted across at least three different years
- `parseSsimDate('01JAN26', { centuryPivot: 50 })` round-trips through `formatSsimDate`
- `parseSsimDate` is unaffected by the ambient locale, asserted against the full locale matrix
- Lowercase or malformed month abbreviations return the sentinel
- `parseDaysOfOperation('1.3.5..')`, `'1 3 5  '` and `'1030500'` each return `[1, 3, 5]`
- `ssimLegTimes` for a 23:40 local departure with `+0900` and a 06:15 arrival with `-0800` and date variation `1` returns instants whose `flightLeg` `dayOffset` is `1`; the same leg with a wrong sign on a variation fails that round-trip, asserted
- `connectionTime` returns `meetsMinimum: false` when available time is below the minimum
- `pnpm run validate` stays green
