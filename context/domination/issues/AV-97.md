# AV-97 — Aviation: SSIM dates

**Scope:** Read and write the `DDMMMYY` date of airline schedule files, with English month letters and a century the caller states.

## Gap

Airline schedules are exchanged in IATA's Standard Schedules Information Manual (SSIM) formats,
and their dates are not ISO 8601. A schedule file's period of operation is written `DDMMMYY`:
two digits of day, the first three letters of the month in English, upper case, and two digits of
year — `01JAN26` (the Chapter 7 layout is not reached; marked, not asserted). Slot messages use the same day and month letters without the year: "The last
three are the first three letters of the name of the month in English: JAN, FEB, …, NOV, DEC".
Schedules are filed against seasons and carry dates a year ahead and decades back
([painpoints § Aviation](../painpoints.md#aviation)).

Two defects follow. A parser that reads the month through the platform's locale breaks on any
machine whose default locale is not English. A parser that picks a century itself is wrong for
either the archive or the forward schedule.

(IATA, _Standard Schedules Information Manual_, Chapter 7 — not reached; marked, not asserted.
[AECFA (Spain), _Quick Guide to Using the IATA SSIM format_, Chapter 6 slot messages](https://www.slotcoordination.es/sites/Satellite?blobcol=urldata&blobkey=id&blobtable=MungoBlobs&blobwhere=1576865330896&ssbinary=true) —
a national coordinator's guide, labelled corroboration for the day and month letters.)

## Scope

- `packages/gmt/src/aviation/parse/parseSsimDate.ts`:
  - `parseSsimDate(value: string, options: TwoDigitYearOptions): string` — `DDMMMYY` to an ISO
    date. Without `options.yearWindow` the result is the sentinel.
- `packages/gmt/src/aviation/format/formatSsimDate.ts`:
  - `formatSsimDate(value: string, options: TwoDigitYearOptions): string` — An ISO date to
    `DDMMMYY`, upper case. Returns the sentinel for a year outside the window, so a round trip never
    changes a century.
- `TwoDigitYearOptions` is the type INT-15 defined; this story adds no type.
- This story opens the `aviation/` realm: the `@northguild/gmt/aviation`,
  `@northguild/gmt/aviation/parse` and `@northguild/gmt/aviation/format` subpaths (`package.json`
  `exports` and `typesVersions`, the dox `gmt-modules.ts` barrel), and the Aviation industry in
  `apps/dox/src/data/industries.json`, with namespace `aviation`.

### What returns the sentinel

- `parseSsimDate`: a value that is not two digits, three upper-case English month letters and two
  digits; lower or mixed case; a month name in another language; a day that does not exist in the
  month and windowed year; no `yearWindow`.
- `formatSsimDate`: a value that is not an ISO 8601 calendar date; a year outside the window; no
  `yearWindow`.

## Tool

**SSIM Date Reader.** Enter a `DDMMMYY` date and a window and see the ISO date; switch the page's
locale and see the answer stay the same. A second panel writes a date back. Calls `parseSsimDate`,
`formatSsimDate`. Teaches: a format's month letters are part of the format, not a translation.

## Design notes

- **The month letters are fixed English and never go through `Intl`.** They are a code, not a
  translation. The twelve are a constant table in the function, and the JSDoc says the function is
  locale-independent: an explicit exception to the library's locale-aware formatting, with a test
  over the full locale matrix proving the ambient locale changes nothing.
- **Upper case only.** Every example in the guides read is upper case, and a reader that accepts
  `jan` invites a writer that emits it.
- **The century is the caller's.** SSIM does not say which century `YY` belongs to, and schedule
  files hold dates both ahead and behind. The caller states `yearWindow`, as INT-15's pattern parsers
  require; the library holds no cut-off of its own.
- **A round trip never changes a century.** `formatSsimDate` refuses a year outside the window.
- **The Chapter 7 text is not reached.** IATA does not publish SSIM openly. The day and the English
  month letters are corroborated by AECFA's guide to Chapter 6; the two-digit year that follows them
  in a Chapter 7 period of operation is not. `/plan-story` cites Chapter 7, or public sources from at
  least two countries, before the function is built, and the JSDoc marks the form until then.
- Seasons, days of operation and leg times are not part of this story.

## Authority — what each rule rests on

IATA is a multinational industry body; SSIM is its standard. The manual is not publicly
available.

| Rule | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Day as two digits, month as three upper-case English letters | AECFA (Spain), _Quick Guide to Using the IATA SSIM format_, Chapter 6: "The first two are the day of the month. Shown as 01, 02,….30, 31"; "The last three are the first three letters of the name of the month in English: JAN, FEB, ……, NOV, DEC". Labelled corroboration; IATA SSIM Chapter 6 not reached |
| Two digits of year after the month in a Chapter 7 period of operation | IATA SSIM Chapter 7 — **not reached; marked, not asserted** |
| No `Intl` for the month | **GMT rule.** The letters are a code fixed in English |
| The century is the caller's | **GMT rule**, INT-15's: no standard names the century of a two-digit year |
| A year outside the window is refused by the formatter | **GMT rule.** Writing it would read back as another year |
| A day that does not exist is rejected | TC39 Temporal `overflow: "reject"`; [coding-standards § Calendar & zone semantics](../../coding-standards.md#calendar--zone-semantics) |
| Sentinels | [coding-standards § API Contract](../../coding-standards.md#api-contract) |

## What gmt provides (do not re-implement)

- `TwoDigitYearOptions` from INT-15 — the caller-stated hundred-year window
- `internal/twoDigitYear.ts` and `internal/yearWindowStart.ts` from INT-15 — placing a two-digit
  year in that window, and reading the option
- `isValidDate` — the ISO date the formatter takes

## Verification

- `parseSsimDate('01JAN26', { yearWindow: 2000 })` returns `2026-01-01`; with no `yearWindow` it returns the sentinel
- `parseSsimDate('29FEB24', { yearWindow: 2000 })` returns `2024-02-29`; `'29FEB25'` and `'31APR26'` return the sentinel
- `parseSsimDate('01jan26', …)`, `'01Jan26'`, `'01JANV26'`, `'1JAN26'` and `'01ENE26'` return the sentinel
- `parseSsimDate` returns the same answer under every locale in the full locale matrix
- `formatSsimDate('2026-10-25', { yearWindow: 2000 })` returns `25OCT26`; `formatSsimDate('1999-12-31', { yearWindow: 2000 })` returns the sentinel
- Round-trip: `parseSsimDate(formatSsimDate(d, w), w)` returns `d` for every date `d` inside the window `w`
- `pnpm run validate` stays green
