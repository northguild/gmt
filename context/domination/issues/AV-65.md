# AV-65 — Aviation: Cumulative flight and duty limits

**Scope:** The rolling-window and calendar-period caps on flight time and duty time for flight crew under 14 CFR Part 117 and EASA ORO.FTL, as interval sums over a crew duty log.

## Gap

AV-27 computes the maximum for one flight duty period. The regulations also cap the *total* — over 7, 14 and 28 days, over a calendar year and over any 12 months — and a crew member can be legal for today's FDP and illegal to fly it because of last month. These limits are deterministic sums over a log, need no table, and were "out of scope" in AV-27 only because that story is about the table lookup.

| Limit | FAA, 14 CFR 117.23 / 117.25 | EASA, ORO.FTL.210 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flight time | 100 hours in any 672 consecutive hours; 1,000 hours in any 365 consecutive calendar days | 100 hours in any 28 consecutive days; 900 hours in any calendar year; 1,000 hours in any 12 consecutive calendar months |
| Flight duty period / duty | 60 FDP hours in any 168 consecutive hours; 190 FDP hours in any 672 consecutive hours | 60 duty hours in any 7 consecutive days; 110 in any 14; 190 in any 28 |
| Recurrent rest | "at least 30 consecutive hours free from all duty within the past 168 consecutive hour period" (§117.25(b)); after more than 168 hours in a theater over 60° away, "a minimum of 56 consecutive hours rest" that "must encompass three physiological nights' rest based on local time" (§117.25(d)) | Recurrent extended recovery rest "shall be 36 hours, including 2 local nights", with no more than 168 hours between the end of one and the start of the next, increased to 2 local days twice every month (ORO.FTL.235(d)) |
| Pre-duty rest | "at least 10 consecutive hours immediately before beginning the reserve or flight duty period", with "a minimum of 8 uninterrupted hours of sleep opportunity" (§117.25(e)) | at home base at least as long as the preceding duty or 12 hours, whichever is greater (ORO.FTL.235(a)); away from base the greater of the preceding duty or 10 hours including an 8-hour sleep opportunity (ORO.FTL.235(b)) |

([14 CFR 117.23](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/14/117.23), [14 CFR 117.25](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/14/117.25), [Commission Regulation (EU) No 83/2014 — ORO.FTL.210 and ORO.FTL.235](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32014R0083))

## Scope

- `packages/gmt/src/aviation/calculate/cumulativeLimits.ts`:
  - `cumulativeLimits(log: CrewDutyEntry[], at: string, options: { regime: 'faa117' | 'easaFtl', referenceZone: string }): { limits: { name: string, clause: string, window: Interval, used: string, maximum: string, remaining: string, exceeded: boolean }[], violations: Violation[] } | null` — Every applicable cap evaluated at an instant, each naming the clause it implements.
  - `projectedLimits(log, plannedDuties: CrewDutyEntry[], options): ReturnType<typeof cumulativeLimits>` — The same, with a proposed roster appended, for pre-assignment checks.
- `packages/gmt/src/aviation/calculate/recurrentRest.ts`:
  - `recurrentRestStatus(log: CrewDutyEntry[], at: string, options: { regime: 'faa117' | 'easaFtl', referenceZone: string }): { satisfied: boolean, lastFreePeriod: Interval | null, localNightsIncluded: number, requiredBy: string }` — FAA: the §117.25(b) 30-in-168 test. EASA: the ORO.FTL.235(d) 36-hour rest including two local nights (AV-27's `localNightOverlap`), no more than 168 hours apart.
  - `preDutyRestSatisfied(log, dutyStart: string, options & { atHomeBase: boolean }): { required: string, actual: string, satisfied: boolean }` — §117.25(e) or ORO.FTL.235(a)/(b), as the regime and location select.
- `CrewDutyEntry` is `{ kind: 'flight' | 'fdp' | 'duty' | 'rest' | 'standby', start: string, end: string }`. `flight` entries are block times from AV-25's `oooiTimes`; `fdp` and `duty` may overlap `flight`, and the sums take the union per kind.

## Design notes

- **Hours-based windows and calendar-based windows are different arithmetic.** "672 consecutive hours" is exact elapsed time and ignores DST; "28 consecutive days", "365 consecutive calendar days", "calendar year" and "12 consecutive calendar months" are local calendar periods in `referenceZone` and are 23 or 25 hours long on transition days. The FAA uses both forms and the EASA text uses calendar forms; each limit's JSDoc states which and cites the clause. Mixing them is the recurring implementation error.
- **The reference zone is the crew member's, not the airport's.** EASA calendar limits run against home-base time; FAA calendar-day limits are evaluated in the operator's designated zone. The option is required so the choice is explicit.
- **Every limit is reported, not just the binding one.** A scheduler needs to see 95 of 100 flight hours and 55 of 60 duty hours together; a boolean cannot show that the roster is legal and fragile.
- **Standby counts by regime.** EASA counts a proportion of airport standby toward duty (ORO.FTL.230); the proportion is an operator-scheme value and is a parameter, documented, never a default.
- Extensions, augmented operations and fatigue-risk-management-system variations are out of scope, as in AV-27; the sums here are the unvaried statutory ones.

## What gmt provides (do not re-implement)

- `oooiTimes` / `flightLeg` from AV-25 — block times for `flight` entries
- `acclimatisedZone` from AV-27 — where a caller wants the reference zone derived rather than stated
- `mergeIntervals` / `sumIntervals` / `intersectIntervals` from CORE-6 — per-kind unions and window intersections
- `bucketRange` / `floorToZone` from CORE-5 — calendar-year, calendar-month and calendar-day windows in the reference zone
- `spanMs` from CORE-2 — elapsed-hour windows

## Verification

- 100 flight hours within 672 hours returns `remaining: 'PT0S'` for that limit and no violation; one more minute is a violation naming §117.23(b)(1)
- A 672-hour window across a fall-back transition is exactly 672 elapsed hours; a 28-day EASA window across the same transition is 28 local days (671 elapsed hours), asserted side by side
- 900 flight hours in a calendar year in `referenceZone` is the limit; the same hours straddling 31 December are not, while the 12-consecutive-month limit still applies to them
- 60 FDP hours in 168 hours under FAA and 60 duty hours in 7 days under EASA differ for a log with duty that is not FDP, asserted
- `recurrentRestStatus` under FAA is `satisfied: false` when no 30-consecutive-hour free period exists in the last 168 hours and reports `requiredBy`; under EASA a 36-hour rest spanning one local night fails and one spanning two passes
- `preDutyRestSatisfied` under EASA at home base requires 12 hours after a 10-hour duty and 13 hours after a 13-hour duty; away from base 10 and 13 respectively; under FAA 10 hours everywhere
- `projectedLimits` with a planned 8-hour flight flags the flight-time limit before the roster is worked
- Overlapping entries of the same kind are unioned, not double-counted, asserted
- `pnpm run validate` stays green
