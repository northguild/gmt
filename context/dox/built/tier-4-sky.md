# Tier 4: the landing sky, stars and bodies

Part of [built/index.md](index.md). The globe it sits behind: [tier-4-globe.md](tier-4-globe.md). The Look at bar, names and events: [tier-4-sky-views.md](tier-4-sky-views.md). Planets, small bodies and close passes: [tier-4-sky-bodies.md](tier-4-sky-bodies.md).

## Stars, band, Sun and Moon

- `HeroSpace.astro` holds three `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none` canvases (`gmt-space.css`): Milky Way, stars, meteor. `lib/sky.ts` (`mountSky`) has no loop of its own: the first `view` makes the first draw and sets `data-sky="ready"`; after that it redraws only when the view's centre moves 0.01 degree, the Earth's centre or radius half a pixel, or zoom 0.002. `skyWithoutGlobe` draws once if the globe fails. Size caps, minified and gzipped: sky 5,216 bytes (the split into `sky-stars`, `sky-band` and `sky-bodies` for the 400-line rule costs about 120), Moon painter 3,200, Sun painter 2,048, meteor 4,096 (`scripts/sky-size.test.ts`).
- Star dots scale with the Earth's on-screen radius (`skyLens`), so a phone's are smaller; `hero-phone` reads it from `data-sky-camera`.
- `lib/sky-math.ts` holds the gnomonic projection, sidereal time (Meeus 12.4) and `starViewMatrix` (IAU 1976 precession). Stars and the Milky Way map are J2000; the Sun, Moon and sidereal time are of date. Tests pin Meeus examples and JPL Horizons rows (`sky-math.test.ts`).
- Stars: HYG v4.4 (pinned to commit `53e3df3`, hash checked), magnitude 5.5 and brighter, 2,865 stars in `src/assets/hero-sky.bin` (budget 24 KB), generated by `scripts/prepare-hero-sky.mjs`; the catalogue is never committed. It is CC BY-SA 4.0: `hero-sky.json` is the share-alike sidecar saying the repo's MIT licence does not cover it, and the hero's "About this sky" button opens the popup that lists "Stars" on `/` only.
- Milky Way: ESO panorama eso0932a (CC BY 4.0, credit "ESO/S. Brunier"), reduced by `scripts/prepare-hero-milkyway.mjs` (run by hand, refuses any other hash) to `src/assets/hero-milkyway.webp` (budget 40 KB). `hero-milkyway.json` is the credit sidecar (CC BY 4.0 asks for the credit as given and a note that the work was changed). A failed fetch or decode leaves the band out; stars still draw. Listed as "Milky Way" after "Stars".
- Sun and Moon draw on the stars canvas. The Moon is seen from the camera (`moonFromCamera`: parallax, size, phase), never from the Earth's centre, and is pinned to nine JPL Horizons observer rows. Pins sit at least 10 degrees from full (the limb is ill-conditioned near full). A body is drawn only inside the camera's field of view, and both sky canvases are clipped outside the Earth's disc.
- Moon map: `src/assets/sky-moon.webp` (1024x512, budget 80 KB) is NASA SVS's CGI Moon Kit `lroc_color_2k.jpg` (LRO LROC data, a national peer, labelled so in `MOON_IMAGERY`; no international body publishes a global mosaic), reduced by `scripts/prepare-sky-bodies.mjs`, which pins URL, bytes and sha256 and refuses any other file. Both prepare scripts read their pinned source through `scripts/prepare-source.mjs`, so the download, the `--<flag> <file>` override and the hash check live once.
- `loadSkyMap(src, signal)` in `lib/space-sky.ts` is the one fetch-and-decode (`createImageBitmap` with `colorSpaceConversion: "none"`); the Milky Way and both bodies use it. `skyInputsOf(holder, host, signal)` reads the page's `data-sky-src`, `data-milky-way`, `data-sun-map` and `data-moon-map` once, so the hero and the deck stage cannot drift. `sampleMap` (`lib/sky-map.ts`) is the one bilinear lookup, wrapping in longitude; the band, the Sun and the Moon use it.
- The body maps load late so the first paint never waits: after `sky.ready` and `whenIdle()`, `loadBodyMaps` walks the `SKY_BODIES` registry in `space-sky.ts` (one row per body: its map input and a `make` that imports the painter chunk), fetches each declared map and calls `sky.maps({ sun, moon })` once with the painters that loaded. A body whose fetch, decode or import fails stays flat while the other still textures; `data-sky-maps` is `ready` when any loaded, else `failed`, with no console error. It is skipped under `forced-colors: active` or an aborted signal. The maps are fetched at `priority: "low"`, and `loadBodyMaps` first awaits `SkyInputs.after`, which the hero sets to the globe's `dawnReady`, so the Earth image is on the GPU before the maps and planet chunk compete with it (a globe that fails to mount resolves it with nothing, and a rejecting `after` never blocks). The deck passes no `after`. `body-paint.ts` (Moon) and `sun-paint.ts` are separate lazy chunks, each with its own cap row in `sky-size.test.ts`, so `sky.ts` carries neither. A new body is a row in `SKY_BODIES`, a painter and a cap row.
- `bodyFrame` (`lib/body-frame.ts`) is a body's IAU WGCCRE pole and prime meridian (Archinal et al. 2018) from its `BodyRotation` row (`lib/body-rotation.ts`: Sun, Moon, Mars, Saturn, Uranus), turned to the frame of date by `precessToDate`; it is pinned to JPL Horizons from the geocentre and from the geostationary camera, where the sub-observer point differs by more than 3 degrees. `createMoonPainter` (`body-paint.ts`, which also holds the helpers both painters share: `discSize`, `coverage`, `screenAxes` and the one-canvas `cachedDisc`) shades with Lommel-Seeliger plus earthshine (the flat Moon's alpha `shine`, decoded to linear light, added on the lit side too so no seam shows at the terminator) over the map's white (its 99.9th percentile, so a full Moon does not clip and the half Moon is darker; the map's tones are remapped in the WebP to the Lunar Sourcebook albedo anchors, cos(lat)-weighted percentiles over |lat| <= 70 degrees), turns the linear level to its sRGB encoding for the alpha (a canvas composites encoded values), tints to `ICE_TINT`, and caches the painted disc by radius, sun direction, sub-observer point and pole angle, so a still sky repaints nothing. Below `MIN_BODY`, or with no map, the flat path draws unchanged (a digest test holds it byte-identical).
- Sun map: `src/assets/sky-sun.webp` (256x256, budget 16 KB) is ESA's press image of Solar Orbiter's PHI (observed 22 March 2023, CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO, labelled in `SUN_IMAGERY`), cropped to 1.02 solar radii and reduced to one grey band by the same script, with the mosaic's tile vignetting divided out (`flattenDisc`: about 2 %, a grid on screen; the limb darkening kept), its white scaled to 255 and stored near-lossless (lossy WebP's blocks showed as a grid) (the SOAR FITS data is CC BY-NC, so none ships). The JPEG is an orange table over the continuum; `sun-colour-map.mjs` stops the script unless every channel of the table rises with brightness, so the grey keeps the continuum's order, and a test holds the shipped disc's rings falling to the limb. Solar north is up in the source. `bodyPole(SUN_ROTATION)` (`body-frame.ts`, IAU 2015 pole of date, pinned to Horizons) gives the disc's turn on screen; `createSunPainter` (`sun-paint.ts`) paints `ICE_TINT * sample / 255`, so nothing clips and the limb darkens, opaque with a coverage edge; it reads two map pixels inside the limb so the black past it draws no dark ring, and the glare stays the flat one. A Sun that is not painted draws the flat core unchanged.
- `SKY_CREDITS` (`lib/data-credits.ts`) is the one ordered list of sky credit rows; the globe popup, the deck's credits and `SkyCredits.astro` read it. `sky.ts` stays under 400 lines by handing the bodies to `sky-bodies.ts`, the band to `sky-band.ts` and the stars to `sky-stars.ts`; their code counts toward the sky's cap because they are imported statically.
- Rejected imagery sources: SOHO (non-commercial terms) and JAXA Kaguya (stricter terms). Reason: only a source whose licence lets the site ship and credit it is used; if ESA's PHI page stops stating CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO, the prepare script's pinned file is not re-fetched until that is settled.

## Loading order

- Load order on `/`, by need: the Earth image, then the zone outlines, then the sky's stars and band, then the Sun and Moon maps and the planet chunk, then the events data (only on reaching the info or Look at button). `Globe.astro` calls `warmGlobe` (`lib/globe-warmup.ts`) for a hero stage: after the first paint (`whenPainted`, `lib/yield-to-main.ts`) it adds `<link rel="preload" as="fetch" crossorigin fetchpriority="high">` for the image (only where `navigator.gpu` exists and `--gmt-globe-imagery-alpha` is above 0) and imports the globe chunks, so the static import chain is walked while the page is idle. The hashed URLs live in `lib/globe-assets.ts`, which both `globe.ts` and the warm-up read. `loadBoundaries` waits for `dawnReady` in the dawn entrance and fetches at `priority: "low"`. The `preloadLandingScripts` integration (`lib/preload-landing-scripts.ts`, on `astro:build:done`, after `pruneWidgetCss`) adds a small inline script to the landing that, at the first contentful paint, appends a `modulepreload` link for every script its entry scripts import statically, so the chain is one round trip, not five; links in the head instead slowed the copy's first paint on a slow connection. Other pages are unchanged. Every file the landing loads sits under `/gmt/_astro/` with a content hash, so `_headers` serves it `immutable`.
