# Tier 2: gates and CI

Part of [built/index.md](index.md). Operator steps (run, baseline, weekly sweep, Firefox): [tier-6-runbooks.md](tier-6-runbooks.md).

## Gates

- Both measuring gates are diagnostics: a few px of browser difference is read, not chased. A run that measured nothing, skipped an interaction or crashed fails.
- `scripts/grow-measure.mjs` (`pnpm run grow:measure`) samples every tool page's height each frame, on load and on input, in Chromium and WebKit at 1440 and 390 px. It asserts an HTTP 200, a matching root, a largest painted jump of 48 px for the root and for `<main>`, every `.gmt-grow` at rest, a final height equal to the reduced-motion run's, and that each requested interaction happened. `rawMaxJump` is reported, never asserted. A tool's `drag` entry names the handle or range; for a custom handle the script reads its parent as the track.
- `scripts/readout-still.mjs` (`pnpm run readout:still`, no arguments) drags every handle by keyboard and pointer in Chromium and WebKit at 1440 and 390 px, plus the widget root forced to 360 and 300 px. It fails if a hero plate, the chart frame, the dragged control or anything above it moves or resizes, a required control is missing, a handle's value does not change, or it checked nothing. `handle-early`, `handle-compare` and the Timetable Reader's `handle-2` to `handle-4` are optional. Only the insides of the day track, the plot and a hold cell's live wrapper may move (`drawn`). An `entries` item takes `values` (strings, or `"options"` for a select) or `chips: true` (a `group` role named by its legend). Pass/fail logic is in `scripts/gate-checks.mjs`, tested by `gate-checks.test.ts`.
- A tool with no drag handle (the decoders, readers, ledgers and checkers) is not in `readout-still`. Its definition of done: across every preset each plate keeps its size and a held region keeps its height. A result region may grow between presets through `.gmt-grow`, which `grow:measure` gates.
- The MAR-16 tools measure the whole widget (`scope: "widget"`, frame `.gmt-gnss-frame`). Only `GNSS_DRAWN` (lanes, bars, strip, plot, rulers, a hold's live wrapper) may move; the range chip is a `riders` element. `textBoxes` excuses code-span width and unbreakable instant pieces, never a frame or plate.
- Trap: Firefox lays out in fractions, so `readout-still` compares raw boxes with a 0.5 px epsilon and never rounds before comparing. It snapshots again after the scroll into view; a box that moved with the scroll is `scroll moved <element>`. Findings group per element.
- Trap: a late writer outside `grow()` (a script setting a height after the region settled) is a jump. `attributeFrames` (`gate-checks.mjs`) splits a sample that spans two frames, and `withJumpDiagnostic` names the `<main>` frame in a `<main>`-only jump.
- `a11y-media` proves `prefers-contrast` applied before reading the border: a probe property under `@media (prefers-contrast: more)` must read `1`, mid-page and in a fresh context with `contrast: "more"`. An unset probe is `emulation-not-applied` (the engine, not the page); a set probe with a weak border token is `contrast-border-token`.
- `scripts/html-diff.mjs` compares built widget markup (a changed widget, a changed page around it, a new widget page with no baseline). `visual:diff` is the pixel gate. `html-diff.mjs` finds a widget root with or without `not-content`.
- `frame-check` (`scripts/frame-check.mjs`, tier `pr`, three engines) serves `dist` itself and checks at 1440 and 390 px: the `/install/` code frame corners, each `/upstream/` card head's icon and badge within 2 px of the title's first-line middle, and Markdown list dashes and ticks within 2 px of the first-line middle (no component list item has a dash). A page with nothing to measure fails.
- `table-fit` (`scripts/table-fit.mjs`, tier `weekly`, three engines) opens every built page with a `<table` (`listTablePages` in `scripts/lib/table-pages.mjs`, read from `dist`, never a fixed count) at 1440 x 900 with every `<details>` open and every tab shown. It fails a scroll box wider than it shows; a table with no scroll box fails against its block and against `main`'s content box and by `scrollWidth` against `clientWidth`. Rule `table-scrolls`. `measureCells` (`scripts/lib/cell-overflow.mjs`, 1440 and a fresh 390 px load) fails a cell that is too small for its content or whose text reaches more than 1.5 px past it (rule `cell-overflows`); it skips visually hidden headers and `.gmt-sr-only` content. A run that measures no table fails. `--shard <i>/<n>` keeps every n-th page of the sorted list (`shardOf` in `scripts/gates/contract.mjs`, also used by the weekly sweep's legs); `--list-pages` still prints every page.
- `contrast-measure` is Chromium only. It clips a glyph rect to each scroller's client box, never its border box.
- `canada-layout-check` takes `--browsers` and `--engine`, checks both clock-change pages in dark and light at 1440, 1024, 420, 375 and 300 px (420 is just above the WebKit stack threshold), and measures the probe table as loaded and with the verdict forced to ok, bad and unreadable. Offsets come from the page's own config through `expectedOffsets` in `scripts/lib/clock-check-harness.mjs`.

## The runner

- `pnpm --filter dox gates` (`scripts/gates.mjs`) runs every gate from one build and one static server and prints one screen. Gates are data in `scripts/gates/registry.mjs` (`id`, `script`, `tier`, `browsers`, `fullBrowsers`, `themes`, `needs`, plus `args`, `base`, `browserArg`, `pages`). `select.mjs` turns changed files and the import graph (`graph.mjs`) into pages; `screen.mjs` prints.
- Tiers nest. `pr`: contrast-measure, grow-measure, readout-still, data-credits-check, esc-cascade, sound-check, rail-stored-still, canada-layout-check, frame-check, a11y-axe, a11y-tab-walk, a11y-media. `weekly` adds canada-clock-still, canada-stored-still, table-fit. `local` adds globe-smoke, html-diff, visual-diff (they need a GPU or a baseline).
- Page contract: every gate script answers `--list-pages` (a JSON array of site paths without `/gmt`, no browser, printed whole however long, since the runner reads it through a pipe), takes `--pages <path,...>` (an unknown path exits 2) and, when it loads pages, `--base <url>`. With `GATE_REPORT` set, each failure goes through `recordFinding` as `{ gate, rule, page, selector, browser, width, theme, message }` (`rule` is a short stable id such as `readout-moved`, never prose), and the run ends with `recordMeasured(gate, n)` (`scripts/gates/findings.mjs`, `contract.mjs`). Exit 0 without that line, or with `n = 0`, is `FAIL measured nothing`; exit 0 with findings is FAIL.
- Page costs: a gate whose pages differ a lot in run time is `costs: true` in the registry and also answers `--list-page-costs` (each path's minutes). `readout-still` is the one: its costs are the `minutes` on each `TOOLS` entry, one WebKit run of that tool on one machine, since only the ratios matter. A page with no cost stops a sharded run.
- `grow-measure`, `readout-still`, `data-credits-check` and the three Canada scripts accept `firefox` in `--browsers`.
- Selection: a changed file selects the pages whose import closure holds it (`.mdx`, `.astro`, `.ts`, `.tsx`, `.mjs`, `.css`; relative paths and `~/`). A change under `packages/gmt/src/<ns>/<module>/` selects the pages that import `@northguild/gmt/<ns>/<module>` plus the sample. Chrome files (`src/styles/*`, Starlight overrides, `astro.config.mjs`, `apps/dox/package.json`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`), any other library change and any `apps/dox/` file no page closure holds select the sample (`SAMPLE` in the registry; `registry.test.ts` asserts each path is in `dist`). A changed gate script runs that gate on the sample; a changed runner file (`scripts/gates/`, `gates.mjs`, `gate-checks.mjs`, `scripts/lib/`) runs every gate on the sample. Over 12 pages the run keeps the sample plus 6 picked evenly; `--all` lifts the cap. Test files and `apps/dox/src/test/` helpers never select a page. With no changed file under `apps/dox/`, `packages/gmt/src/` or `pnpm-lock.yaml`, the runner prints `gates: PASS  nothing to check` and does not build.
- An empty run is not a pass: when the diff selects pages but no requested browser can run an applicable gate (`emptyRunOutcome` in `gates/select.mjs`), `gates` exits 2. `contrast-measure` is Chromium only, so `--browsers firefox` alone runs nothing for it.

## CI jobs for Dox

- `dox-site` ("Dox site build") builds `@northguild/gmt` and the site once on Node 22 with `fetch-depth: 0` and uploads the `dox-site` artifact. It runs when `determine-affected` selects `dox-dist` or `dox-tables`, and on every PR reporting `dox_changed`. The artifact is one tar of `apps/dox/dist`, every gitignored generated file (`src/generated/`, reference, changelog and industry pages under `src/content/docs/`, `public/llms*.txt`) and every tracked file the build rewrote; `dist/` alone is not enough because gate selection reads `src/content/docs/**`. The pack step passes NUL-delimited names to `tar --null -T -`. It keeps 14 days with `overwrite: true` (`download-artifact` keeps the first of two same-named artifacts, #380). A failed `dox-site` skips every job that needs it.
- `consistency` keeps only fast checks and builds nothing.
- `dox-browser` unpacks the artifact, builds `@northguild/gmt`, and runs `test:dist` (`DOX_DIST_REQUIRED=1`), the browser tests and `frame-check`, on the `dox-dist` and `dox-tables` checks.
- `dox-table-fit` runs `table-fit` in four shards ("Dox tables fit (shard 1/4)" to "4/4"), Chromium only, `fail-fast` off. The `dox-tables` check (`scripts/lib/affected.mjs`) runs on every Dox change and every `packages/gmt/src` change (`GMT_LIB`) and always opens every table page: a shared change can spill a table on a page it never touched. A shard that measures no table fails.
- `dox-gates` runs on PRs reporting `dox_changed`, after `dox-site`, as five legs, each `gates --tier pr --browsers chromium --themes dark,light --no-build --against origin/<base>`. The runner exits 2 if `dist/gmt/index.html` is missing. Gates stay serial within a leg because the clock-still gates flake when gates share a runner. The legs are `readout-still` (`--only readout-still`, job 40 min and step 35; one page takes 4m35s, with the Timetable Reader 25m37s), `contrast` (`--only contrast-measure`), `canada` (`--only canada-layout-check,data-credits-check,a11y-media,frame-check`), `esc-grow` (`--only esc-cascade,grow-measure`) and `other` (`--skip` the gates the others name, so a gate added later lands here). Non-`readout-still` legs have 20 / 15 minute job / step limits. `scripts/gates/ci-legs.test.ts` reads `ci.yml` and fails when a `pr`-tier gate runs in no leg or two. The runner prints its screen at the end and appends it to `$GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY` (`gates/step-summary.mjs`). Each gate logs to `.gates/`; a leg's step stops 5 minutes before its job so a failed leg uploads `apps/dox/.gates/` (without `vite-cache/`) as `dox-gates-<leg>-<run id>`.
- The `dox-gates` jobs are advisory until the owner makes them required. A PR touching nothing under `apps/dox/` or `pnpm-lock.yaml` skips them; a lockfile change alone runs them (Playwright or axe-core may bump).
- One Dox PR runs up to 11 Dox jobs against the organisation's 20 concurrent jobs.
